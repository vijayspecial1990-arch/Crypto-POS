Crypto POS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Who we are

Crypto POS (“we”, “us”) provides software that helps merchants create cryptocurrency payment requests, track payment status, reconcile balances, and request payouts. Crypto POS is not a bank, exchange, custodian, or money transmitter.

2. Data we process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Account email and authentication identifiers
  • Business profile details (name, phone, country, address, and related fields you provide)
  • Payment metadata (amounts, currencies, statuses, invoice/order IDs, customer name/email if entered)
  • Payout requests (amount, network, destination wallet address, notes, status)
  • Notifications and limited device/session data needed to operate the service

We do not store customer wallet private keys.

3. Why we process it

We process this information to:

  • Authenticate merchants and keep accounts secure
  • Create and track payments
  • Calculate fees, balances, and payouts
  • Send limited transactional emails (welcome, optional paid-sale notices, payout confirmations)
  • Prevent abuse and provide customer support

4. Processors / service providers

We use trusted providers to operate Crypto POS:

  • Clerk — authentication (including email one-time codes)
  • Supabase — application database and backend functions
  • Resend — transactional email (sent from payments@keyoai.net; replies to hello@inbound.keyoai.net)
  • NOWPayments — cryptocurrency payment processing and checkout

Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies in addition to this one.

5. Fees and payments note

Crypto conversion and on-chain settlement are handled by NOWPayments. Crypto POS may charge a platform commission on payout amounts as described in the app. Payment and ledger records support accounting, dispute handling, and legal requirements.

6. Retention

We retain payment and ledger records as needed for accounting, disputes, and legal obligations. You may request account closure; some records may be kept where required by law.

7. Security

API secrets are stored encrypted server-side. Staff PINs (if enabled) are stored hashed on-device. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity and business account before fulfilling a request.

9. Children

Crypto POS is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Crypto POS after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

For privacy requests, email hello@inbound.keyoai.net with your business name and account email.
